Infrared Heaters

Infrared heaters are a type of electric heater that use infrared radiation to warm up a space. Unlike conventional space heaters that warm the air directly, infrared heaters emit infrared rays, which are absorbed by objects, walls, and people in the room. These objects then radiate heat back into the air, creating a more direct and efficient heating experience. Infrared heating technology is based on the principle of how the sun heats the Earth—by sending infrared radiation that warms objects and people, rather than just the air around them. This makes infrared heaters more efficient and effective in many situations compared to traditional convection heaters.


Convention Heaters

Convection heaters are a type of space heater that work by warming up the air in a room and then circulating it throughout the space. The process of convection involves heating the air directly, which then rises and displaces cooler air, creating a continuous flow of warm air. This is the most common type of heating used in homes, offices, and other indoor spaces. Convection heating systems rely on the principle of natural air movement. As the air is heated by the heater, it becomes lighter and rises, while cooler air is drawn in to replace it. This cycle continues, creating a gentle and even distribution of heat.


Radiant Heaters

Radiant heaters are a type of space heater that use infrared radiation to provide direct heat to objects and people in a room, rather than heating the air. These heaters emit infrared radiation, which is absorbed by surfaces such as walls, floors, furniture, and even people. The heated objects then re-radiate the absorbed heat into the surrounding air, creating a comfortable and efficient warming experience. Radiant heaters are often used in situations where focused or spot heating is required, such as in personal spaces, outdoor areas, or specific rooms in a home or office. They are known for their ability to deliver quick, direct warmth without the need to heat the air first.


Thermal Wave Heaters

Thermal Wave heaters are a type of infrared heater that uses advanced infrared technology to generate heat and provide efficient, comfortable warmth. These heaters are often designed to produce a steady, uniform heat distribution through the use of specialized ceramic elements or other advanced materials that optimize the infrared radiation process. Thermal Wave heaters are widely known for their energy efficiency, ease of use, and ability to provide a more natural, pleasant form of heating. The term "Thermal Wave" can refer to the combination of both radiant (infrared) heat and some level of convection to circulate heat more evenly in a space.


Baseboard Heaters

Baseboard heaters are a type of electric or hydronic heating system designed to provide warmth to a room by heating the air near the floor. They are typically installed along the base of walls in a room, where they take advantage of natural convection to distribute heat evenly. Baseboard heaters can either be electric (using electrical resistance to generate heat) or hydronic (using hot water heated by a boiler). They are a popular choice for homes, offices, and commercial spaces due to their simplicity, effectiveness, and space-saving design.
