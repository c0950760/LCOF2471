Who We Are

At ThermoNest, we’re passionate about creating innovative, energy-efficient space heaters that bring comfort and warmth into your home or workspace. We believe that heating solutions should be more than just functional—they should enhance your environment, reduce your carbon footprint, and make every space feel cozy, no matter the season. Founded with a commitment to excellence and sustainability, ThermoNest is driven by a vision to revolutionize the way people experience warmth. Our team of engineers, designers, and energy experts works tirelessly to develop cutting-edge heating technology that blends modern design, reliable performance, and eco-friendly features. We understand the importance of comfort, especially in today’s fast-paced world, and we strive to provide the best heating solutions that meet the needs of diverse lifestyles and spaces. We specialize in creating high-quality space heaters—from ceramic and convection heaters to radiant and infrared models—all crafted with advanced technologies that prioritize energy efficiency, safety, and ease of use. Whether you need an effective heater for your home office, bedroom, living room, or commercial space, we have a product that fits your needs. At ThermoNest, we believe that great products come from great ideas. Our designs are centered around providing fast, effective heat without compromising the environment or your energy bill. As we continue to innovate, we remain dedicated to creating heating solutions that help you enjoy maximum comfort, with minimal energy consumption and environmental impact. Our mission is simple: to make warmth effortless, sustainable, and stylish.

Our Story

Founded in 1975, ThermoNest began with a simple idea: to create space heaters that not only provide warmth but also make a positive impact on the environment and your life. What started as a small project between a group of engineers passionate about energy conservation has grown into one of the leading brands in the space heater industry. Today, ThermoNest continues to lead the way in designing innovative, sustainable, and user-friendly heating products. We are excited to continue growing and evolving as we work towards creating the next generation of energy-efficient heating solutions. Whether you're looking to keep your living room warm on a chilly night or need a full-home heating system, ThermoNest has you covered. Thank you for choosing us as your trusted partner in home comfort. Together, we can make your space warmer, greener, and more efficient—one heater at a time.
