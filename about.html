About ThermoNest Solutions Inc.

Your Trusted Partner in Home Comfort

Welcome to ThermoNest Solutions Inc., At ThermoNest, we believe that warmth is more than just a temperature—it’s an experience that brings comfort, safety, and well-being to your home. As innovators in the field of space heating technology, we are passionate about providing energy-efficient, reliable, and high-performance heating solutions that transform the way you experience warmth. With years of experience, we strive to provide the best machines, customer service, and value to our customers worldwide.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to create advanced, sustainable space heaters that not only keep you warm but also contribute to a more energy-conscious world. By combining cutting-edge technology with superior design, we aim to enhance comfort while reducing your energy bills and environmental impact. Whether you're heating a single room, a larger space, or a whole home, ThermoNest has a solution that fits your needs.

What We Do ?

At ThermoNest, we design and manufacture a wide range of space heaters, from portable models to fixed, whole-home heating systems. Our products are crafted with the latest in heating technology, ensuring that every unit is energy-efficient, long-lasting, and easy to use. We specialize in:

  1. Portable Space Heaters: Compact, efficient, and easy to move, these heaters are perfect for providing instant warmth to specific rooms or areas.
  2. Smart Thermostats: Control your ThermoNest heater remotely and set schedules for ultimate convenience and energy savings.
  3. Whole-Home Heating Solutions: From baseboard heaters to radiant systems, we offer integrated solutions to keep your entire home cozy and energy-efficient.
  4. Environmentally-Friendly Heating: We are committed to reducing the carbon footprint with heaters that use sustainable energy sources and low emissions.

Our Story

Founded in 1975, ThermoNest began with a simple idea: to create space heaters that not only provide warmth but also make a positive impact on the environment and your life. What started as a small project between a group of engineers passionate about energy conservation has grown into one of the leading brands in the space heater industry. Today, ThermoNest continues to lead the way in designing innovative, sustainable, and user-friendly heating products.
